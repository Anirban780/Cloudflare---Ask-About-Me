# About Anirban Sarkar

Anirban Sarkar is an adaptable and results-driven engineer specializing in **Software Engineering, Data Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure, and DevOps**. He builds scalable distributed systems, high-throughput data processing pipelines, and modern cloud-native architectures that deliver high reliability and low latency.

## Professional Focus & Goals

Anirban is currently targeting roles as a **Software Engineer, Data Engineer, Cloud Engineer, or DevOps Engineer**. He thrives in environments that value sound engineering fundamentals, automated testing, continuous integration, and clean architectural design. 

He is passionate about modern cloud platforms — particularly edge computing with Cloudflare Workers, distributed state synchronization using Durable Objects, vector retrieval systems, and serverless workflows. He believes in building systems that are simple to reason about, self-healing, and observable under production workloads.

## Core Technical Philosophy

1. **Grounded Systems Design:** Build software around clear contracts, structured schemas, and type safety across every layer from the database to the user interface.
2. **Data Hygiene & Idempotency:** Treat data consistency as paramount. Data pipelines and ingestion workflows must be deterministic and fully idempotent to safely tolerate transient failures.
3. **Automated Verification:** Software is only complete when backed by comprehensive unit, integration, and property-based tests.
4. **Pragmatic Tooling:** Prioritize native platform primitives that eliminate operational friction and unnecessary external dependencies.

## Engineering Collaboration & Problem Solving

Anirban approaches complex technical problems by breaking them down into decoupled, verifiable components. Whether collaborating with cross-functional teams or driving autonomous engineering initiatives, he prioritizes open technical documentation, thorough design reviews, and rapid feedback loops. He believes that maintainable codebases are built through consistent conventions, automated checks, and empathetic communication across team members.
